An unofficial fan game. Worm and Ward are by Wildbow (J.C. McCrae); read them free at parahumans.wordpress.com and parahumans.net. Cape details adapted from the Worm Wiki (CC BY-SA).

How to play

Every day there's a new cape from Worm or Ward to identify, in three files.

Dossier

The file starts fully redacted. Each wrong guess removes one black bar, in this order: . You have 7 guesses.

Lineup

Guess any cape and see how they compare with the target on the same six details. A green check means it matches. An amber mark means close: the power classes overlap. A red cross means no match. Guess as many times as you need.

Power file

Read a description of the cape's power with their names blacked out. Wrong guesses open extra notes about the cape.

A few rules

  • Names, cape names and civilian names all count. Skitter, Weaver and Taylor Hebert are the same answer.
  • Status means where things stand at the end of Ward. Team means the last team the cape belonged to.
  • New files arrive at midnight CET. Missed one? Open it from the archive.
  • Wormdle is in beta: not every cape's file is finished yet. Capes with unfinished files can still be guessed, but some of their details show as Unknown.

Your record

Wormdle is in beta

The cape files are still being written. Right now some of the capes on record have complete files. Only those can be a day's answer, and only those give reliable results when you guess them.

You can still guess any cape. If its file isn't finished, some of its details show as Unknown, so a guess may not compare the way you'd expect.

Spoilers for all of Worm and Ward

The answers come from both stories, start to finish. The clues include who ends up dead, which side a cape ends up on, and secret identities. If you're still reading, you may want to come back later.
