How to play

Every day there's a new cape from Worm or Ward to identify, in two files.

Lineup

Guess any cape and see how they compare with the target on seven details. A green check means it matches. Amber means close: for Power class, the cape shares a power class with the target but its main class is different; for First appearance, the cape first appears within 2 arcs of the target. On First appearance, an arrow shows whether the target first appears later (▲) or earlier (▼), counting through Worm, PRT Quest, Glow-worm and Ward in reading order. A red cross means no match. Guess as many times as you need. Wrong guesses open notes: after 2, which story introduced the cape (Worm or Ward), and after 4, whether they're a cluster cape. Stuck? After 8 guesses you can reveal the answer, though the file then counts as unsolved.

Power file

Read a description of the cape's power with their names blacked out. Wrong guesses open extra notes about the cape. You have 7 guesses.

A few rules

  • Names, cape names and civilian names all count. Skitter, Weaver and Taylor Hebert are the same answer.
  • Status means where things stand at the end of Ward. Team means the last team the cape belonged to.
  • "Introduced in" counts characters introduced in Glow-worm as introduced in Ward.
  • New files arrive at midnight CET. Missed one? Open it from the archive.
  • Wormdle is in beta: not every cape's file is finished yet. Capes with unfinished files can still be guessed, but some of their details show as "Not on file".
  • Found a bug, spotted a wrong detail, or have a suggestion? Email contact@raunaksarbajna.com.

First appearance

Every guess now shows the arc where that cape first appears. An arrow tells you whether the subject first appears later (▲) or earlier (▼), and the tile turns amber when you're within 2 arcs. Arcs run on in reading order from Worm through PRT Quest and Glow-worm into Ward.

Also new this week

  • After 2 wrong guesses, Introduced in tells you whether the cape first appeared in Worm or Ward. Characters from Glow-worm count as Ward.
  • After 4 wrong guesses, Cluster cape tells you whether they're part of a cluster.
  • Truly stuck? After 8 guesses, you can reveal the answer. The file counts as unsolved.

Wormdle is in beta

The cape files are still being written. Right now some of the capes on record have complete files. Only those can be a day's answer, and only those give reliable results when you guess them.

You can still guess any cape. If its file isn't finished, some of its details show as "Not on file", so a guess may not compare the way you'd expect.

Spoilers for all of Worm and Ward

The answers come from both stories, start to finish. The clues include who ends up dead, which side a cape ends up on, and secret identities. If you're still reading, you may want to come back later.
